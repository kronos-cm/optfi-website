/ 05 Plain answers

The questions that actually matter.

If we haven't answered yours, write to team@optfi.eu.

Can I use OptFi today?

Not yet. OptFi is in canary — a constrained live phase under hard allocation caps. The platform must prove its own economics before broader access. Join the waitlist for first invitation when canary clears its proof window.

What does the waitlist actually get me?

Three things: priority access when canary graduates, monthly operator letters with the real numbers (not marketing decks), and an invitation to the private quarterly walkthrough where strategy decisions get explained on the record.

Why Germany-first?

Germany has one of the strictest crypto tax frameworks in the world. Building for the hardest accounting environment first means the economics are real — not inflated by ignoring tax drag. The platform generalises outward; it doesn't generalise inward.

Why not DeFi yet?

DeFi execution is deferred until it can demonstrably beat the best centralised path on after-tax, after-fee compounding with a clean audit trail. That bar hasn't been cleared. We track opportunity and risk data — execution waits.

What stops the platform from cutting corners on its own gates?

Gates are enforced in code, not policy. Every promotion attempt is logged. Any transaction above threshold requires a hardware-wallet signature. The operator cannot bypass the audit log — and the audit log is what defines the system's word.
