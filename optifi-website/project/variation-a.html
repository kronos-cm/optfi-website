01The problem

Gross returns lie. Compounding is what survives the tax bill.

Most crypto products optimise for screenshots: big green numbers, paper APYs, narrative tokens. Almost none of them account for the boring, brutal arithmetic that decides whether you actually end up wealthier — fees, slippage, signing risk, and the way Germany taxes every disposal.

What most platforms show you

  • ×Gross APR with no fee, slippage, or tax line. The number you'd take home is materially lower.
  • ×In-sample backtests tuned to look great on the chart that ships in the marketing deck.
  • ×Custodial control with a "trust us" wallet model and no public audit trail.
  • ×One-click yield on protocols that haven't survived a single risk-off cycle.

What OptFi commits to instead

  • ✓After-tax, after-fee compounding as the only metric that promotes a strategy to the next stage.
  • ✓Walk-forward proof on out-of-sample data before a candidate is allowed near a real order.
  • ✓Hardware-wallet signing gate on every transaction above a configured notional threshold.
  • ✓Public audit trail of every decision, gate evaluation, and signing event.

05Plain answers

The questions that actually matter.

Can I use OptFi today?

Not yet. OptFi is in canary — a constrained live-trading phase under strict allocation caps. The platform must prove its own economics before broader access is opened. Join the waitlist and you'll get the first invitation when canary clears its proof window.

What does the waitlist actually get me?

Three things: priority access when canary graduates, monthly operator letters with the real numbers (not marketing decks), and an invitation to the private quarterly walkthrough where strategy decisions get explained on the record.

Why Germany-first?

Germany has one of the strictest crypto tax frameworks in the world. Building for the hardest accounting environment first means the economics are real — not inflated by ignoring tax drag. The platform generalises outward; it doesn't generalise inward.

Why not DeFi yet?

DeFi execution is deferred until it can demonstrably beat the best centralised path on after-tax, after-fee compounding while preserving a full audit trail. That bar hasn't been cleared. We track DeFi opportunity data and risk scores — but execution waits.

What stops the platform from cutting corners on its own gates?

The gates are enforced in code, not policy. Every promotion attempt is logged. Any transaction above threshold requires a hardware-wallet signature. The operator cannot bypass the audit log, and the audit log is what defines the system's word.
